Modifications: the keyed sampler is transcribed from Triton into CUDA C++ with
native pointer-based inputs and outputs. The RoCE integration replaces
Python/Torch bindings with an exception-safe C ABI, explicit stream ownership,
and Rust runtime management. The communication protocol is retained.

The RoCE portions additionally retain the attribution to b12x's RoCEnante,
Copyright 2026 Luke Alonso and the b12x contributors, under Apache-2.0.

The sampler's log/exp routines and tables derive from Arm Optimized Routines,
Copyright (c) Arm Limited. The MIT option is included in Arm-MIT.txt.
